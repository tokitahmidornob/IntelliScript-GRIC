The Critical Failures of the Status Quo

1. Clinical Blindness

Prescribing in a data vacuum without verified access to a patient's active medication list or allergy history.

2. The Handwritten Hazard

Illegible penmanship forces pharmacists to "guess" dosages (e.g. 10mg vs 70mg), leading to systemic toxicity before the first dose is finished.

3. Lethal Drug-Drug Interactions (DDI)

The current paper system lacks a 'Real-Time Interceptor,' allowing doctors to accidentally prescribe conflicting medications that cause systemic organ failure.

4. Unnecessary Testing

Lost paper records force doctors to re-order exact same tests (X-rays, ECGs), creating a massive financial drain on vulnerable families.
